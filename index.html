OUR PHILOSOPHY

A research-oriented approach to translation

“Translation, for us, is not a profit-making material business; a conscious-experimental Research exercise it is.”

ITG’s published philosophy emphasizes meaning, cultural context, linguistic nuance, timely communication and value for clients—not mere word-for-word substitution.
