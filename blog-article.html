TRANSLATION PRACTICE

Meaning goes beyond individual words

Translation is a contextual task. Terms may have different meanings depending on the domain, intended audience, tone and cultural setting. A literal word-for-word substitution does not always carry the same meaning into the target language.

The value of a skilled language specialist

A human translator can review linguistic register, idioms, technical terminology and the goals of the original writer. Editing and proofreading provide an additional opportunity to catch inconsistencies.

Automated tools can assist with drafting and repetitive tasks, but the appropriate level of human review depends on the document, its purpose and its sensitivity.

Indian language considerations

Indian languages and dialects are diverse in grammar, script and regional usage. A project brief should specify the language, target readership, preferred script and any terminology requirements.

For legal, medical or similarly consequential documents, engage qualified domain reviewers and confirm the requirements of the receiving institution.
