OUR STORY

About Indian Translators Group

Indian Translators Group (ITG) is a Wardha-based translation and language-services organization established in July 2009, built around human translation, cultural understanding and research-led communication.

Request a Quote →Contact ITG

THE ORGANIZATION

Who we are

According to the legacy company information, ITG is a privately held online translation agency based in Wardha, Maharashtra, India. The organization focuses on high-quality professional human translation with special attention to time, value and contextual accuracy.

Its positioning highlights scholar-translators, linguistic sensitivity and deep awareness of Indian-language pragmatics, helping clients communicate naturally across audiences, domains and scripts.

PHILOSOPHY

What drives ITG

“Translation, for us, is not a profit-making material business; a conscious-experimental Research exercise it is.”

This philosophy remains central to ITG’s brand identity: language work should preserve meaning, register, intent and cultural depth while remaining practical, timely and economically sensible for clients.

CAPABILITIES

LANGUAGE COVERAGE

Indian languages and beyond

The older ITG site references service coverage across English, Hindi and many Indian languages including Marathi, Gujarati, Odiya, Assamese, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Telugu, Sanskrit, Bodo, Konkani, Sindhi, Urdu and Maithili, with additional regional dialect support subject to project review.
